# nRF board archive

Frozen fabrication packages: exactly what was uploaded to the fab, one folder per order.
Nothing here changes after the order; the live design is generated in [`../`](../).
Whether a revision turned out to work is a status line in its section below.

## `2026-10-10-rev-a/`

**Status: received, flashed over SWD, Bluetooth and button wake work.** Two boards, JLCPCB
Standard PCBA, assembly on both sides. Shutter on the camera not tested yet.

`J1` on rev A is 1 camera ground, 2 not connected, 3 S1, 4 S2, so the camera cable crosses
its first two wires:

| `J1` | Camera port |
|---|---|
| 1 | 2 (GND) |
| 2 | 1 (6 V), or leave the wire unconnected |
| 3 | 3 (S1) |
| 4 | 4 (S2) |

The fabrication package for **rev A** (E73-2G4M08S1F nRF54L15 module, CR2032, Q1
reverse-cell FET, 220 Ω relay drive), generated by `../tools/regen.sh` at commit `a6036b5`.

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `openrz67-nrf-gerber.zip` | Gerber and drill files |
| `openrz67-nrf-bom.csv` | BOM with LCSC numbers |
| `openrz67-nrf-pos.csv` | CPL |
| `openrz67-nrf-schematic.pdf` | schematic |
